# Human-First Content Writer (The Evidence-First Editorial System)

A production-grade editorial system engineered to produce authoritative, high-converting, and genuinely natural content. Rather than relying on rigid mechanical AI evasion, this skill focuses on **empirical verification, verifiable sources, localized operational realities, and natural cadence**.

---

## ⚖️ The 6-Tier Editorial Priority Framework

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. Research Accuracy & Citation Integrity      (30%)  │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. Search Intent & Direct Value               (20%)  │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. Granular Specificity & Real Mechanisms     (20%)  │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. Natural Voice & Organic Flow               (15%)  │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 5. Technical & Entity-Driven SEO              (10%)  │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 6. Subtle AI-Slop Cleanup                      (5%)  │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 1. Research Accuracy & Citation Integrity (30%) — The Core Anchor
- **No Floating Statistics:** Never state a percentage, timeline, or ranking without naming the institution, survey, or source (e.g. Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics, WHO Database, municipal gazettes).
- **Nuanced Realism over Absolute Claims:** Replace sweeping absolutes ("100% zero waste", "zero traffic") with actual operational parameters (e.g. "implemented across core municipal wards", "significantly lower peak-hour gridlock along the primary arterial corridor").
- **Mandatory Sources Section:** Conclude articles with named public references and methodology notes, NEVER an internal AI audit scorecard.

### 2. Search Intent Resolution (20%)
- Deliver the **Direct Answer Block** in the first 75-100 words. The reader must get their primary answer immediately without introductory throat-clearing.

### 3. Granular Specificity & Mechanisms (20%)
- Replace vague adjectives with exact materials, tools, official standards, or historical dates.
- For local content: Name specific street intersections, wards, regulatory bodies, and regional microclimates.

### 4. Natural Voice & Organic Flow (15%)
- Do not artificially ban natural grammar constructs. Writers use em-dashes and compound sentences when rhythm demands it.
- Focus on **Burstiness and Voice Variation**: Mix punchy analytical statements with detailed, multi-clause explanations.

### 5. Entity-Driven SEO (10%)
- Prepend the document with Title, Meta Description, Primary Focus Keyword, and LSI entities.
- Structure headings (`H2`, `H3`) to match topical search journeys.

### 6. Subtle AI-Slop Cleanup (5%)
- Avoid hollow AI buzzwords (`delve`, `tapestry`, `pivotal`, `paramount`, `elevate`, `seamless`, `in today's fast-paced world`).
- Cut empty introductory setup lines and generic "In conclusion" recaps.

---

## 📌 Deliverable Output Contract

Every content generation MUST:
1. Deliver the final copy into a dedicated `.md` file in the project workspace.
2. Include the metadata frontmatter at the top (Title, Meta, Focus Keyword).
3. Conclude with a verified **Sources & References** section.
4. **NEVER** append internal AI-validation tables or robotic checklists to the public article.
